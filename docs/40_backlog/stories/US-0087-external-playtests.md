## Description

Three sessions with players who are NOT the team, fully logged. The M6 exit criterion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Three sessions, four to six external players each, three matches per session.
- [ ] Brief is 60 seconds MAXIMUM. If more explanation is needed, that is the finding.
- [ ] All twelve questions asked individually and IN WRITING, never aloud in the group.
- [ ] Logged to `docs/40_backlog/playtests/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with telemetry attached.
- [ ] Q5 scores BELOW Q4 across sessions.
- [ ] Q7 at least 4 of 5; Q12 at least 70 percent.
- [ ] The turn observed in at least two of three sessions.
- [ ] TEL-LOBBY-FILL-TIME recorded even though lobbies are hand-assembled.

## Test notes

18 player-matches is enough for hunt duration and life length; almost certainly NOT enough for
the patient-versus-aggressive ratio. Record that limitation rather than over-claiming.

## Notes

Facilitated sessions differ from the real thing. The turn happening for a group who can hear each
other laugh does not prove it happens for strangers — that stays open past M6.

If the turn does not happen, no amount of content or population fixes it. That is what this
corpus exists to detect early and cheaply.
